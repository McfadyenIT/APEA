APEA AI Performance Engineering architecture: an orchestrated pipeline from recording and browser-context analysis through parameterization, generation, validation, execution and root-cause analysis, with a shared Browser Context Graph, knowledge, memory, platform registry and self-repair layers.

APEA — AI Performance Engineering Architecture

Recording-first · orchestrated · self-healing · now with first-class browser-interaction support (iFrames, Shadow DOM, popups, OAuth / 3DS, payment gateways)
Orchestration
APEA Orchestrator · Decision Engine
Routes every pipeline stage, decides CONTINUE / RETRY / INVOKE-CLAUDE / STOP / ESCALATE, and consults the Knowledge Base, Memory and Platform Registry at each step. Coordinates the closed-loop self-repair.
